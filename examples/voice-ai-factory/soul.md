# Voice-First AI Factory Landing

## Purpose

Build a landing that feels like an AI Factory: the visitor speaks or types what they want to build; the page listens first, then visually assembles a production-ready AI workflow.

Positioning:

> We help companies take AI from idea or prototype into production workflows that survive real business operations.

Emotional purpose:

> People should arrive curious and leave feeling like builders.

## Audience

- Business owners who need AI but are unsure where to start
- Teams with PoCs that never reached production
- International clients who care about operational outcomes

Audience mindset: interested but overwhelmed; need to be listened to before being sold.

## Constraints

- Bootstrap via client projects — landing is a sales + capability demo
- Do not start with a generic service list
- One-screen voice-controlled flow first; scroll only for secondary content
- English for v1
- Avoid purple-gradient AI clichés; soft blues + paper neutrals

## UI Principles

- Listen first
- Waveform stays present across stages
- Sketch → color reveal communicates readiness
- Factory metaphor: modular, premium, not cartoon industrial

## Locked Direction

- Name: Sketch to Color
- Navigation: one-screen voice-controlled
- Motion: GSAP stage transitions + waveform movement
- 3D: Three.js isometric stage graphics
- CTA: Book a Production Readiness Call (Calendly placeholder)

## Non-Goals

- Real microphone permissions in v1 (simulate voice)
- Full multilingual toggle in v1
- Traditional multi-section agency brochure as the primary experience
